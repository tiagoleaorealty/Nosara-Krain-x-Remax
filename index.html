01

An asking price that has not sold in thirty months is not a valuation.

Whatever Green Sanctuary is worth, the market has now spent two and a half years declining to pay $1.85M for it. Pricing above that means asking a buyer to clear a bar nobody in this corridor has cleared.

02

Land does not scale price here the way it should.

Jawa Ecolodge sits on 5,020 m², almost exactly your parcel, with two structures, 385 m² built, five bedrooms, a pool, ocean views, its own well, solar and gated security. It is asking $1,600,000 and has been for twenty months. Jawa carries four times Green Sanctuary's land and asks a quarter of a million less. In this market buyers pay for built area, income and walkability.

03

Income is the one lever that moves your number up.

The Guiones compound asks $1,724,995 on roughly $154,000 of documented gross income, close to eleven times gross. That is the highest defensible ask on the board precisely because the number is proven rather than projected. Clean revenue records from your homes could move your range materially, and however far back they go, it is the fastest work we can do together.
